# US & Global Finance Brief

English briefing adapted from howard0928/news-distribution_public. Selects at most 6 significant stories from the past 24 hours, with source links, financial implications and a dated VIX reading.

Covers US and global macroeconomics, central banks, equities, bonds, currencies, commodities, banking, earnings and major transactions. Dedicated Google News RSS searches include Axios, WSJ, Reuters, FT and CNBC, alongside general finance searches, GDELT and filtered Fed/BLS releases. Source availability varies; no publisher quota or filler. Summaries use accessible headlines/snippets, without bypassing paywalls. Publisher links may require a subscription and Google News links may redirect.

Keep existing Actions secrets: OPENAI_API_KEY, OPENAI_MODEL, EMAIL_HOST, EMAIL_PORT, EMAIL_USER, EMAIL_PASSWORD, EMAIL_TO, EMAIL_FROM. Never commit credentials. Gmail uses smtp.gmail.com:587 and a Google-generated App Password.

Schedule: daily at 07:00 America/Los_Angeles, following daylight saving time. GitHub may delay runs and email arrives after processing; exact 07:00 delivery is not guaranteed. GitHub can disable public repository schedules after 60 days without activity. Manual runs remain available and each sends an email.

Install requirements.txt plus pytest. Run `python -m pytest tests -q`. Preview with `python -m src.main --dry-run`; send with `python -m src.main`. Dry-run supports heuristic fallback without an API key. Verify a live Actions run after publishing.

## Market and macro dashboard

Adds dated daily market levels, prior-session and approximately one-month trends: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, Hang Seng, Nikkei and FTSE; all 11 US sector ETF proxies; aggregate, Treasury and corporate bond ETF proxies; gold futures and major FX pairs. FRED supplies Treasury yields, Fed target bounds/effective rate and investment-grade/high-yield option-adjusted spreads. Macro includes NFP monthly job changes, headline/core CPI and PCE, PPI final demand, retail sales, GDP and unemployment. Rate changes use basis points; inflation includes monthly and yearly changes. Dates are observation periods rather than release dates. Missing/stale data are labeled; no numerical values are generated by AI.

ADP, ISM and FOMC have official release links and explicit news searches; no structured ADP/ISM numeric feed is configured. ETF proxies are labeled as such, and the dashboard increases email length beyond the original five-minute news-only target.


## Timestamps and market analysis update

Story times are preserved from source metadata through ranking and summarization, then converted to Pacific time with PST/PDT labels. RSS update-only times and GDELT first-seen times are labeled separately; they are not represented as original publication times. Missing or ambiguous times are labeled. AI does not generate timestamps.

Each story includes a short Market impact section covering relevant bond tenors/prices/yields, equity sectors, currency pairs and commodities. The prompt requires conditional implications to be distinguished from observed reactions. Analysis depends on accessible headlines/snippets and is not a full-article or real-time market study.

The dashboard now includes VIX, dollar index and WTI oil futures. Watch today selects up to three moves exceeding explicit editorial screens in src/dashboard.py, ranked by magnitude relative to those screens. These thresholds are heuristic, not statistical significance or trading advice. Stale/missing data are excluded; each highlighted move includes an observation date and conditional economic transmission channel. The full dashboard retains the original broad coverage and is longer than a five-minute read; the news section is capped at six stories to control length.
